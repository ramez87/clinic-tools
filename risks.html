Procedure risks

Literature rates for counseling: natural history and treatment risks by disease. Not NMH data. Numbers are averages from mixed populations; adjust for the patient in front of you.

Definitions. Any complication: any procedure-related event, including temporary ones. Major: permanent new deficit or death from the procedure, unless the row says otherwise. Death: procedure-related unless labeled all-cause. Rates are not head-to-head unless the source is a randomized trial.

Sources: claude/procedural-risk-reference.md and the Procedural Risk Quoting Reference doc. Data version 2026-10-21.
