Modified Rankin Scale (mRS)

Global disability after stroke, hemorrhage or treatment. Use the interview questions, or pick the grade directly. Used across aneurysm, AVM, stroke and carotid visits. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

mRS--
For the note:

van Swieten JC, et al. Stroke 1988;19:604-7. Good outcome is usually defined as mRS 0-2.

Interview: branching structured mRS questions (3 questions per patient). Question 1 splits independent (mRS 0-2) from dependent (mRS 3-5). Grade 6 is chosen directly. "Sit up in bed without help" stands in for mRS 5 (bedridden, incontinent, constant care); use the direct pick if it does not fit.
