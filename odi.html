Back pain score and Oswestry Disability Index

For vertebral augmentation and basivertebral nerve ablation visits. Short item labels; patients complete the full form. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

ODI--
For the note:

ODI scoring: each section 0-5; total divided by the possible score (50, or 45 if one section is not answered) x 100. Bands (Fairbank 2000): 0-20% minimal, 21-40% moderate, 41-60% severe, 61-80% very severe, 81-100% extreme (bed-bound). Minimal detectable change 10 points.

Fairbank JC, Pynsent PB. The Oswestry Disability Index. Spine 2000;25:2940-52. Item labels are shortened here; use the licensed full questionnaire with patients.
