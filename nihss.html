NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS)

For stroke, carotid and intracranial stenosis visits. Go in order and do not go back to change a score. Score what the patient does on the first attempt, not what you think they can do, and do not coach. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

NIHSS--
For the note:

Cards: the picture, naming objects, sentences and word list are on the official NIHSS cards; have them ready (or screen-share them for telehealth). Telehealth: items needing touch (sensory) or close assessment may be limited; note this.

Total 0-42. Severity: 0 none, 1-4 minor, 5-15 moderate, 16-20 moderate to severe, 21-42 severe. Untestable (UN) items are not added to the total.

Brott T, et al. Stroke 1989;20:864-70. Follow the official NIH Stroke Scale instructions for each item.
