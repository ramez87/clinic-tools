NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS)

For stroke, carotid and intracranial stenosis visits. Score what the patient does on the first attempt. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

NIHSS--
For the note:

Total 0-42. Severity: 0 none, 1-4 minor, 5-15 moderate, 16-20 moderate to severe, 21-42 severe. Untestable (UN) items are not added to the total.

Brott T, et al. Stroke 1989;20:864-70. Follow the official NIH Stroke Scale instructions for each item.
