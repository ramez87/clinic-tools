Special situations
Restart window--
    For the note:

    Step 1, DVT prophylaxis: compression devices from day 1; prophylactic heparin or enoxaparin 24-48 hours after onset once repeat CT is stable (SAH: after the aneurysm is secured; craniotomy or EVD: per neurosurgery).

    Doses: prophylaxis enoxaparin 40 mg daily (CrCl under 30: 30 mg) or heparin 5,000 units SC every 8 hours. Apixaban 5 mg twice daily (2.5 mg if 2 of: age 80 or more, weight 60 kg or less, creatinine 1.5 or more) or rivaroxaban 20 mg daily with the evening meal (15 mg if CrCl 50 or less). Heparin drip about 12 units/kg/hr, aPTT 50-70 s or anti-Xa 0.3-0.5, NMH nomogram.

    Source: NMH neurointerventional follow-up protocol, section 26. Clinical judgment and the prescribing team decide each case.
