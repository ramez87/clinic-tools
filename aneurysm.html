PHASES (Greving et al., Lancet Neurol 2014): population, hypertension, age, size, earlier SAH, site; 5-year absolute rupture risk by total score. Developed in intradural aneurysms.

ISUIA (Wiebers et al., Lancet 2003): 5-year cumulative rupture rates by size and location. Anterior circulation (ICA, ACA/ACom, MCA): 0%, 2.6%, 14.5%, 40% for <7, 7–12, 13–24, ≥25 mm. Posterior circulation and PCom: 2.5%, 14.5%, 18.4%, 50%. Prior SAH from another aneurysm raises the <7 mm rates to 1.5% (anterior) and 3.4% (posterior). Cavernous carotid: 0%, 0%, 3.0%, 6.4%.

Annual risk = 1 − (1 − 5-year risk)^(1/5). Lifetime risk = 1 − (1 − annual risk)^(remaining years), assuming a constant annual risk. These are population estimates; they do not include shape, growth or other risk factors, and extrapolating 5-year data over decades adds uncertainty.
