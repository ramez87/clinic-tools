Neurointerventional clinic tools

Visit scores: what nurses and staff record at each visit. Planning tools: grading, risks and decisions for the assessment and plan. Nothing entered here is saved or sent anywhere.

Pulsatile tinnitus, IIH and CSF leak

Tinnitus Handicap Inventory (THI)
25 questions, total 0–100 with grade. HIT-6 headache score
Headache Impact Test total and impact category.

Back pain

Back pain score (NRS) and ODI
NRS 0–10 (equivalent to VAS) and Oswestry Disability Index, with change from the last visit.

Stroke, aneurysm and AVM visits

Modified Rankin Scale (mRS)
Disability 0–6, with a picture of each grade. NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS)
15 items, total 0–42 with severity.

Risks and natural history

Procedure risks and natural history
Complications, major complications, death and retreatment by disease and treatment, with a patient view for screenshare.

Aneurysm

Unruptured aneurysm rupture risk
PHASES, ISUIA and estimated lifetime risk, with text for the note.

AVM and dural AV fistula

AVM grading
Spetzler-Martin, Spetzler-Ponce and Lawton-Young supplementary grade. Borden and Cognard
Classify from the angiogram; flags cortical venous drainage.

Spinal CSF leak

Bern score
Brain MRI score for spontaneous intracranial hypotension.

Bleeds and blood thinners

Blood thinner restart after a brain bleed
SDH, intraparenchymal hemorrhage or secured SAH: restart window and dates from our protocol.
