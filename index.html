Neurointerventional clinic tools

Scoring tools for clinic visits. Nothing entered here is saved or sent anywhere.

Aneurysm

Unruptured aneurysm rupture risk
PHASES, ISUIA and estimated lifetime risk, with text for the note.
Procedure risks
Risks by disease, status and treatment. Coming soon.

Bleeds and blood thinners

Blood thinner restart after a brain bleed
SDH, intraparenchymal hemorrhage or secured SAH: restart window and dates from our protocol.

Brain AVM

AVM grading
Spetzler-Martin, Spetzler-Ponce and Lawton-Young supplementary grade.

Dural AV fistula

Borden and Cognard
Classify from the angiogram; flags cortical venous drainage.

Pulsatile tinnitus and IIH

Tinnitus Handicap Inventory (THI)
25 questions, total 0–100 with grade. HIT-6
Headache Impact Test, for IIH headache before and after stenting.

Spinal CSF leak

Bern score
Brain MRI score for spontaneous intracranial hypotension. HIT-6
Headache Impact Test total and impact category.

Vertebral augmentation and basivertebral nerve ablation

Back pain score (NRS) and ODI
NRS 0–10 (equivalent to VAS) and Oswestry Disability Index, with change from the last visit.

Stroke, carotid and intracranial stenosis

NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS)
15 items, total 0–42 with severity. Modified Rankin Scale (mRS)
Disability 0–6; also for aneurysm and AVM outcomes. Barthel Index
Daily activities 0–100, for the 3-month stroke visit.
