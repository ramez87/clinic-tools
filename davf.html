Dural AV fistula: Borden and Cognard

Classify from the angiogram. Any cortical venous drainage (Borden II-III, Cognard IIb and above) is high grade. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

Classification--
For the note:

Borden JA, et al. J Neurosurg 1995;82:166-79. Cognard C, et al. Radiology 1995;194:671-80.

Natural history (internal reference): low grade progressed to cortical venous drainage in about 2% over about 3 years (CONDOR 2022); high grade events about 1.4%/yr if found without symptoms vs about 19%/yr if symptomatic (Strom 2009).
