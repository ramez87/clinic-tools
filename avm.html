Brain AVM grading

Spetzler-Martin grade, Spetzler-Ponce class and Lawton-Young supplementary grade. Nothing entered is saved or sent.

Spetzler-Martin--
For the note:

Spetzler RF, Martin NA. J Neurosurg 1986;65:476-83. Spetzler-Ponce class: A = grade I-II, B = III, C = IV-V (Spetzler RF, Ponce FA. J Neurosurg 2011;114:842-9).

Lawton MT, et al. Neurosurgery 2010;66:702-13: supplementary grade 1-6; a combined Spetzler-Martin + supplementary grade of 6 or less was associated with acceptably low surgical risk in that series.
